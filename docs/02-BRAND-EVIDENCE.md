# 02 — BRAND EVIDENCE: 411 BURGUER
**Document Status:** Complete (Phase 01)  
**Methodology:** Visual Semiotic & Material Extraction (Evidence-Led, Non-Prescriptive)  
**Client:** 411 BURGUER

---

## 1. Visual Evidence Sources

The visual identity of **411 BURGUER** has been extracted exclusively from observed physical and digital touchpoints:
1. **Official Brand Emblem (`imgi_2`):** Primary circular mark, high-contrast serif numeral typography, star symbol, and olive-gold circular badge.
2. **Physical Tableware & Packaging (`imgi_70`, `imgi_71`, `imgi_41`):** Custom laser-engraved bamboo skewers ("411 Burguer"), branded illustration parchment paper, stainless steel serving pans.
3. **Physical Interior & Exterior Architecture (`imgi_9`, `imgi_10`, `imgi_25`):** Exposed rustic brickwork, dark chalkboard menu slates, natural wood sidewalk deck, outdoor parasols, and neighborhood greenery.
4. **Culinary Materiality (`imgi_15`, `imgi_27`, `imgi_41`, `imgi_49`):** Golden glazed brioche crusts, melted cheddar cheese, homemade green herb mayonnaise, crispy fried cheese discs, fresh lettuce and tomatoes.

### 2.1 Primary Brand Tones
- **Carbon Black (`#0E0E0E` / `#161616`):**
  - *Source:* Background and horizontal banner of the official logo emblem (`imgi_2`), evening dining ambiance.
  - *Role:* Provides high contrast, grounded sophistication, and nighttime burger identity.
- **Antique Olive / Vintage Muted Brass (`#7C783D` / `#8A874B`):**
  - *Source:* Circular seal of the official brandmark (`imgi_2`) and social media badge overlays (`imgi_25`, `imgi_31`, `imgi_32`, `imgi_50`).
  - *Role:* Distinctive brand identifier separating 411 Burguer from cliché fast-food yellow/red schemes.

### 2.2 Secondary & Environmental Tones
- **Golden Brioche / Toasted Amber (`#DDA15E` / `#BC6C25`):**
  - *Source:* Glazed brioche buns, golden fried onion rings, toasted parmesan crusted buns (`imgi_15`, `imgi_25`, `imgi_27`).
  - *Role:* Warmth, appetite stimulation, artisanal baking cues.
- **Chalkboard Slate Green (`#25332B` / `#2E3D35`):**
  - *Source:* Classroom-style green chalkboard menu in dining area (`imgi_10`, `imgi_70`) and fresh herb mayo tones.
  - *Role:* Daytime bistro/comfort food cue, organic craft feel.
- **Warm Parchment / Off-White (`#F7F4EB` / `#EBE6DA`):**
  - *Source:* Custom burger wrapping paper and natural daylight (`imgi_41`).
  - *Role:* Clean surface backgrounds, editorial legibility.

### 2.3 Accent Tones
- **Delivery Crimson / Tomato Red (`#C93B2B` / `#B71C1C`):**
  - *Source:* "DELIVERY ON" physical/digital badge toggle (`imgi_25`, `imgi_27`), sliced fresh tomatoes.
  - *Role:* High-visibility conversion CTA moments and urgent order triggers.
- **Melted Cheddar Gold (`#F4A81E` / `#E59800`):**
  - *Source:* Flowing melted cheddar cheese layers on burgers (`imgi_15`, `imgi_49`).
  - *Role:* Visual richness, culinary indulgence highlights.

## 3. Typography Personality Evidence

### 3.1 Display & Brand Numerals ("411")
- **Observed Characteristics:**
  - High-contrast Modern / Didone Serif.
  - Sharp, unbracketed/bracketed thin hairlines contrasted against thick vertical downstrokes.
  - Elegant, classic proportions with architectural stability.
  - Communicates heritage, craftsmanship, and neighborhood confidence.
- **Candidate Typeface Archetypes:** *Playfair Display*, *Bodoni*, *DM Serif Display*, *Prata*.

### 3.2 Sub-Header & Badge Labels ("BURGUER", "HAMBURGUERIA ARTESANAL")
- **Observed Characteristics:**
  - Condensed serif capitals with tight tracking and balanced verticality.
  - Communicates artisanal authenticity and vintage culinary heritage.

### 3.3 Functional & Menu Copy
- **Observed Characteristics:**
  - Clean, legible sans-serif for ingredients, weights (140g), opening hours, and delivery phone numbers (`imgi_15`, `imgi_70`).

## 4. Graphic Motifs & Geometric Language

1. **The Circular Emblem / Heritage Seal:**
   - Circular badge bisected by a prominent horizontal banner containing the numeric address `411`.
   - Balanced upper and lower arches holding brand descriptors.
2. **The 8-Point Star / Sparkle:**
   - Small cardinal star centered above the "BURGUER" mark in the official emblem, adding a subtle touch of quality and craft pride.
3. **Laser-Engraved Bamboo Flags:**
   - Rectangular wooden picks stamped with "411 Burguer" holding burgers together, providing a tactile, authentic serving signature.
4. **Doodle Illustrated Wrapping Paper:**
   - Hand-drawn outline sketches of burgers, sodas, and fries printed across the protective parchment paper.
5. **Chalkboard Hand-Lettering:**
   - Informal, welcoming cursive and block chalk script used for daily lunch specials (*"Prato do dia"*).

## 5. Materiality & Texture Evidence

- **Exposed Rustic Brickwork:** Warm terracotta and aged mortar visible on interior dining walls (`imgi_25`).
- **Dark Slate / Blackboard:** Textured matte surfaces conveying bistro authenticity (`imgi_10`, `imgi_70`).
- **Natural Wood Grain:** Slatted outdoor deck tables and hardwood serving boards (`imgi_9`, `imgi_31`, `imgi_71`).
- **Brushed Aluminum & Stainless Steel:** Individual pasta serving pans and commercial kitchen hardware (`imgi_10`, `imgi_49`).
- **Translucent Food Parchment:** Lightweight greaseproof paper lining trays and baskets (`imgi_41`).

---

## 6. Photographic Personality Evidence

- **Honest & Unfiltered Craft:** The food is photographed as genuinely served—dripping with homemade sauces, glossy freshly toasted buns, and molten cheeses.
- **Dual Ambiance:**
  - *Daytime / Outdoor:* Natural daylight, sidewalk terrace greenery, casual neighborhood bistro atmosphere (`imgi_9`, `imgi_10`).
  - *Evening / Indoor:* Warm ambient lighting, shallow depth-of-field, glowing bokeh, and rich contrast suitable for late-night cravings (`imgi_25`, `imgi_41`).
- **No Stock Artificiality:** Every image is grounded in the real kitchen and physical tables of Rua Botucatu, 411.
